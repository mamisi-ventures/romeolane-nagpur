Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Romeo Lane Nagpur is owned and operated by Mamisi Ventures Private Limited ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Romeo Lane website (www.romeolanenagpur.com) and the Romeo Lane app collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to everyone who uses them.

What we collect

  • Table reservations, guestlists, tickets and private-event enquiries: your name, mobile number, date, time, number of guests, occasion and any requests you add.
  • Lane Club membership: your name, mobile number, and, if you choose to share them, your birthday and anniversary. When you settle a bill we record the bill amount to credit your points, and we keep a record of points, vouchers and redemptions.
  • Your marketing choice: whether you agreed to receive invitations and member offers on WhatsApp and SMS.
  • On your device: the app and website save your booking references in your browser's local storage so you can see your bookings again, and keep a copy of the site's pages and images so it opens quickly and works offline. This stays on your device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment card details through the website or app. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To confirm, manage and follow up on your bookings and enquiries, including contacting you by phone or WhatsApp about them.
  • To run the Lane Club: crediting points, issuing vouchers and birthday or anniversary treats.
  • To send invitations and offers, only if you agreed. You can stop these at any time.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse, for example limiting how many requests one number can make.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Your information is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in Mumbai, India. Only Romeo Lane staff with a staff login can see bookings and member records. We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers. We share it only with service providers who help us run the service (such as our database host), or where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept for up to two years for our records and then deleted. Lane Club records are kept while your membership is active, and deleted within 90 days after you ask us to close it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you, to close your Lane Club membership, or to stop marketing messages. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may also withdraw consent and raise a grievance. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within 30 days.

Age

Romeo Lane serves alcohol. The Lane Club and our events are intended for adults of legal drinking age in Maharashtra. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be shown on the website.
