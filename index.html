Lane Club terms
  1. Points are earned on the food and beverage value of the bill, before taxes and service charge.
  2. Points are redeemed in blocks of 500 for ₹500 off a bill, and are valid for 12 months from the date earned.
  3. Tiers are set by the points you have earned since joining: Gold from 1,000 and Black from 3,000.
  4. Vouchers are valid for 30 days from issue. One voucher per bill, not combinable with other offers or party packages.
  5. Birthday and anniversary rewards are issued for the calendar month and may require photo ID.
  6. Alcoholic rewards are served only to members of legal drinking age.
  7. Membership is personal to the registered mobile number. Romeo Lane may amend the programme with notice.

Hours

Before you visit

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual, every evening. Right of admission is reserved.

Where do I park?

Valet parking is available at JK Heights. Take the lift to the sixth floor.

How are table requests confirmed?

Every request is reviewed by our team. Its status appears under My bookings on the Reserve tab, and you can cancel it there while it is open. For anything urgent, call +91 89565 80661 or +91 89565 80662.

Can I host a private celebration?

Yes, from 15 guests. Choose one of five packages on the Private events tab, compose the menu and send an enquiry. A 50% non-refundable deposit confirms the date; the balance is due before the event.

How do Lane Club points work?

Mention your registered mobile number when you settle. You earn one point for every ₹10 on food and beverage, before taxes, and redeem 500 points for ₹500 off.

Do you serve alcohol to everyone?

Alcohol is served only to guests of legal drinking age, as per government norms. Photo ID may be requested.
